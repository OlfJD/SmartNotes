Westeros Note Skin

Reference for a Game of Thrones note skin, built the same way as the Elden Ring one: a surface, house colours in the colour picker instead of plain colours, and a title font. Answer with a letter, the houses you want and a font number, for example "B, all houses, F1". Nothing is coded yet.

What defines the look

The show's visual language comes from a few recurring things: the opening titles (a brass and iron clockwork map whose cities rise out of the ground under an astrolabe), the painted parchment map of Westeros, house banners in two strong colours with a sigil and house words, and the Night's Watch black against the ice of the Wall. The title lettering is a wide, chiselled Roman capital in the Trajan family.

Colour per house is the natural replacement for the colour picker, the way each Great Rune replaced a colour in the Elden Ring skin.

Same rule as for Elden Ring: the show's logo, title font, artwork and the official sigil drawings belong to HBO and George R. R. Martin. I would draw our own sigils and use Cinzel (free, Trajan-like) instead of the show's lettering. This board shows each house's colours and words; the sigils themselves would be drawn in the next round, once you have picked a direction.
